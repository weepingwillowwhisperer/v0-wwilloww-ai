About

We make the resource you wish someone had quietly handed you before the semester got loud.

wwilloww.ai began with a simple question: what would academic support look like if it assumed an ADHD, autistic, dyslexic, dyspraxic, chronically tired, or twice-exceptional brain from the first draft?

Every guide is written as a practical tool, not a pep talk. The goal is to make the next academic action smaller, clearer, and kinder: an email you can send, a reading system you can restart, a study plan that leaves room for sensory load and recovery.

We are a tiny independent studio. Quiet by design, plain on purpose, and committed to accessible PDFs as the product itself.

FAQ

Common questions, plainly answered.

Don't see yours? Email hello@wwilloww.ai.

When does the first PDF launch? ＋

The First Five Minutes Kit goes out to waitlist subscribers first. The remaining six titles will follow in small batches so each release can be checked for accessibility and revised from reader feedback.

Are the PDFs really accessible, or is "accessible" just marketing? ＋

Every PDF is planned with tagged document structure, a logical reading order, plain-language section summaries, and alt text for every meaningful image. Before selling a title, we will check screen-reader flow and publish plain accessibility notes. If something fails for you, we want to know and we will fix it.

Will there be print-friendly and screen-friendly versions? ＋

Yes. Each title ships in A4 and US Letter, with a high-contrast print variant and a low-stimulation screen variant. The screen variant avoids large solid colour blocks and uses a calmer palette.

Who is wwilloww.ai for, exactly? ＋

Students with ADHD, autism, dyslexia, dyspraxia, dyscalculia, Tourette's, and other neurodivergent profiles — diagnosed or self-identifying — from high school through postdoctoral work. Allistic students, instructors, and disability services staff are very welcome too; the materials were designed to be useful, not gatekept.

How will pricing work? ＋

Individual PDFs will be modestly priced, with a name-your-price option that goes down to free for students who need it — no form, no proof, no questions. Institutional licences for disability services teams will be available at a separate rate.

Is there an AI chatbot or training-data thing here? ＋

No. The .ai in the name is a domain choice, not a product. The PDFs are written by humans, reviewed by humans, and tested by neurodivergent humans. We do not sell your data and we do not train models on subscriber content.

Accessibility statement

Accessibility is the product, not a footer link.

We design wwilloww.ai PDFs and this site to meet WCAG 2.2 AA as a floor, not a ceiling. In practice, that means:

  • Tagged PDF structure with a logical reading order and bookmarks.
  • Plain-language summaries at the top of every section.
  • Alt text for all meaningful images and diagrams; decorative imagery is marked as such.
  • 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, visible keyboard focus, and no information conveyed by colour alone.
  • A reduced-motion mode that honours prefers-reduced-motion. Nothing autoplays, nothing flashes.
  • A dark mode that respects your system setting.

If a PDF, page, or form ever blocks you, email access@wwilloww.ai and we'll respond within five working days — with a fix or an honest timeline.
